# TC SIM historical start source register

Accessed 26 September 2026. The game labels factual context inline on each event card. The player's personal story, choices, effects and outcomes are fictional; no causal or quantitative claim is made about household experience.

| Event | Institutional source and supported claim |
| --- | --- |
| 1980: economic structural change | CBRT, *Dünden Bugüne Türkiye Cumhuriyet Merkez Bankası*, https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/3fac8ff1-5cbe-4c05-89b1-4f0a4cfbaa90/dundenbugune_TCMB.pdf — 24 January 1980 decisions initiated structural change in the economy. |
| 1994: financial crisis | CBRT thesis, *1994 ve 2001 Yılı Ekonomik Krizlerinin Türkiye Ekonomisi Üzerindeki Etkileri*, https://www3.tcmb.gov.tr/kutuphane/TURKCE/tezler/hulyaardic.pdf — identifies and analyzes the 1994 and 2001 crises. |
| 2000: exchange-rate-based stabilization program | CBRT, *Merkez Bankası Tarihçesi*, https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/tr/tcmb%2Btr/main%2Bmenu/banka%2Bhakkinda/tarihce — documents the 2000 program and subsequent monetary-policy transition. |
| 2001: crisis and exchange-rate regime change | CBRT, *Para Politikası Çerçevesi*, https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB%20TR/Main%20Menu/Temel%20Faaliyetler/Para%20Politikasi/Para%20Politikasi%20Cerceve — states that after the 2001 crisis the exchange-rate-based program ended and floating exchange rates began. |
| 2005: six-zero currency redenomination | CBRT, *Merkez Bankası Tarihçesi*, https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/tr/tcmb%2Btr/main%2Bmenu/banka%2Bhakkinda/tarihce — six zeros were removed and New Turkish Lira entered circulation on 1 January 2005. |
| 2008: global financial crisis | CBRT, *2009 Yıllık Rapor*, https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/95d30d54-335f-4540-847d-9068a4322c69/09turkce.pdf — records the crisis that deepened from late 2008 and its effects on economic activity. |
| 2020: pandemic economic disruption | World Bank, *Turkey Economic Monitor* press release, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2020/08/11/in-turkey-continued-containment-of-virus-support-to-vulnerable-households-and-effective-economic-policy-mix-key-to-building-sustained-recovery-says-world-bank — discusses pandemic effects and Turkey's economic response. |

All other dated decision cards are marked in the game as fictional life prompts and make no specific historical-event claim. Numerical effects are game-scale adjustments, not historical wages, prices, exchange rates, or causal estimates. The weekly calendar compresses time; final-year echoes are reconciled into the 1 January 2026 summary. No political party/person, slogan, logo, brand, real photograph, or audio is used.
