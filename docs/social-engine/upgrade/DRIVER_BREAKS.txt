Tracked source unchanged. Geometry probes only; no prototype represented as gameplay.

Finding: a meal-break scheduling condition alone cannot safely implement the seven
permanent drivers. Existing parking/unparking transitions snap vehicle locations
by 1–1.5 metres; strict validation has only canonical-lane or fully-parked states.
There is no persisted legal parking maneuver, bay approach/return path, merge
reservation, or NPC-to-parked-vehicle association after driverId is cleared.

Measured geometry:
- Market approach (31,17) -> bay (31,16), direct loop 0 only. One nominal free
  slot is (32.2,16), 1.2m longitudinally from the rental. mayAdvanceVehicle rejects
  both approach and endpoint with no pedestrians/other cars in the probe. Reusing
  the player parking command would bypass this actual physical guard.
- East approach (44,25) -> bay (45,25), loops 1 and 3. Two nominal slots. Safe exit
  to market follows ~40.64m of pedestrian routing one way.
- Coast approach (29,53) -> bay (29,54.5), loop 1. One slot; ~52.96m walking one way.
- Loops 2 have no direct existing bay. A directed route is possible: at (19,51),
  depart the westbound loop-2 edge onto loop 1 southbound, reach coastal entry
  (29,53). Returning to loop 2 requires another legal routed junction maneuver.
  Current NPC traffic only advances its fixed loop; it does not plan these edges.
- There are four nominal free slots for seven drivers, but the nominal market
  free slot fails the current collision guard. Capacity is not a substitute for
  a collision-valid oriented bay and approach path.
- n84 has 0 while simit costs 3129 in the matched post-recovery snapshot. The
  seven drivers retain assorted ordinary job contracts, not seven paid driving
  contracts. They cannot all be fed by inventing a taxi wage or free meal.

Bounded follow-up design (no general traffic rewrite required):
1. Author collision-valid bay slots, ingress and egress connectors. Treat the
   blocked market second slot as unavailable until its footprint is corrected.
2. Persist a typed DriverBreak record: personId, vehicleId, phase, parkingId,
   baySlotId, route edge IDs/cursor, connector progress, original cruise route
   and speed, expiry and last completed meal tick. Use stages:
   seekBay -> routeToBay -> parkIn -> walkToMarket -> queueAndEat -> walkBack ->
   mergeOut -> driving. Cancellation is explicit if the vehicle/person disappears.
3. Admit a driver only with an open, stocked market and sufficient actual wallet
   funds. Stagger requests with existing seeded identity; reserve a bay only on
   a bounded near approach, with expiry. A full lot means continue legally or wait
   at an authored holding point, never stop inside a junction/crosswalk.
4. Drive planned directed edges under existing signals and body clearance. Sweep
   a real parking curve at low speed, with the driver attached until the car is
   fully parked. Extend strict pose validation to the typed connector phase;
   ordinary off-lane poses remain invalid.
5. Choose a walkable, unoccupied door-side egress. Preserve the parked car and its
   driver association. Use pedestrianPath and the actual market FIFO, wallet,
   stock and ledger. Record a real meal settlement before requesting return.
   Closed/empty/unaffordable service causes bounded retry or an actual unfed
   return; it must not set hunger directly.
6. Walk back to that same vehicle. Check availability/ownership; merge physically
   through egress only after a gap and right of way exist. Restore cruise intent
   after safe entry. Player use/theft, death, stock loss and missing funds have
   explicit cancellation paths, not forced reassignment or blind traffic resets.
7. Separately decide the lawful income/relief policy for the zero-wallet driver.
   Releasing a person to their existing paid job is grounded; replacement-driver
   coverage is optional if reduced ambient traffic during a break is acceptable.
   Actual paid driver employment would require a deliberate economy contract.

Focused acceptance needed before implementation integration:
- All four route loops reach a real eligible bay or remain safely queued.
- Saturated bays, rental occupancy and a player car cannot be overwritten.
- Every ingress/egress tick is collision-valid, including signal changes and
  walking crossings; no teleport occurs at park/unpark boundaries.
- A meal lowers hunger only via an actual market settlement and stock debit.
- Zero wallet, closed market, empty stock and stolen vehicle do not fabricate
  success or strand a person/vehicle reservation.
- Save/load in every phase gives exact sequential canonical replay; quantized
  authoritative angles remain identical in Chromium, Firefox, WebKit and Node.
