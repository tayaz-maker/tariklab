## How the pictures are made

- Every shape is hand-written SVG in `scripts/darbe-h-card-art/motifs.mjs` and
  `scripts/duel-card-art/motifs.mjs`. No image file, image model, screenshot,
  stock photo, map tile, logo or reference picture is opened or traced.
- A card's picture is chosen from the card itself: its series picks the
  setting (ballot room, backstage corridor, tea house, harbour…), keywords in
  its name pick the subject, kind and level pick emphasis (spotlight, warning
  cue for traps). A per-card seed only nudges placement and light.
- Figures are featureless silhouettes. There is no text, real person, party
  symbol, flag, logo or real map in any picture.
- Chromium only rasterises the SVG to lossy WebP; the output is plain VP8.

## What did not change

Card data, rules, deck lists, save keys, file paths and dimensions, the
manifest schema, DARBE-H!'s card frame (colours, labels, `data-*` contract)
and every UI layout. `scripts/duel-art.test.mjs` and
`scripts/darbe-h-art.test.mjs` check coverage, uniqueness, dimensions and that
DARBE-H! cards embed only their own code-drawn art.
