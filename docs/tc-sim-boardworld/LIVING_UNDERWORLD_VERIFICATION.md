  public services and social venues. Opening hours, daily capacity, real calendar
  conflicts, funds and actor status constrain actions. Arrival and earned wages
  complete after time passes. Regional employers fund persistent contracts and
  skill-dependent progression. Paid work continues when bounded career progress saturates; distinct district conditions affect rents,
  commercial demand, stock, services and recruitment.
- Seven organizations use finite treasury and stock, named rosters, leadership,
  recruitment, player tasks, trust, responsibility, promotion, leadership support,
  exit, rivalry, negotiation and alliances. Their daily production, trade,
  payroll, disputes and unlawful transfers continue away from the player. Staff
  generations retain distinct historical identities. Existing firm cash and
  stock are the buyer authority for organization deliveries.
- Voluntary player incidents and scheduled NPC incidents transfer actual stock
  and create incident-linked records. Corroboration requires an actually staffed
  receiving shift. Investigation, consultation and review distinguish suspicion
  from a finding; one uncorroborated record acquits, a corroborated record
  convicts. Explicit fines, compensation and restrictions settle once. Unseen
  evidence is neither displayed nor revealed by consultation availability.
  Public NPC decisions cannot become the player's payable claim.
- Existing family, career, property, needs, history and institutional authority
  remain in the same world. Personal guilt, rank, trust and fines do not transfer
  to a successor; documented estate compensation remains attributable. Departing
  staff reserve their own money for pending cases and never charge replacements.
- The optional domains remain absent from initial authentic v6/v7 saves and pure
  views. Materialized domains use the new capability envelope and canonical
  living slots inside the existing lock/transaction/generation protocol. Old
  clients cannot overwrite the new world or silently restore an older branch.
  A single Gold engine owns time, including exact organization task deadlines.
