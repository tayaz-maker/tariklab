### Shared current-place and calendar recovery policy

`expansion.world.region` is the regional authority. Gold `location` and `city.venue` remain preserved İstanbul anchors; away from İstanbul they must not be presented as the player's actual current place. Do not invent regional districts or mutate old saves merely to render a map.

Existing commitments retain their identity, dates and history across relocation. Remote cancellation, invitation decline and recurrence stop remain available under their ordinary temporal conditions. Physical attendance and İstanbul-local actions are unavailable away from İstanbul. New proposals, acceptance and rescheduling must share one policy across options, preview and resolver: allow only demonstrably feasible travel, or explicitly reject unsupported remote planning. Being away today is not by itself proof that a future İstanbul appointment is impossible. Until a true intercity planning model exists, use the conservative explicit restriction rather than silently confirming a plan using the stale İstanbul anchor. Do not erase commitments, teleport the player, reserve incompatible schedules or charge travel twice. The regional list filter alone is not an authority boundary because direct calendar commands bypass it.
