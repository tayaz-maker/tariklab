### Implemented society hooks and public data

The existing world scheduler calls `societyContact(s,from,to,{kind,start,end,place,amount?},api)` only after a real social, reconciliation, favor or repayment outcome. `societyNpcOptions(s,id)` returns pure, bounded utility candidates; `societyNpcOutcome(s,id,plan,api)` returns whether it handled a conflict-resolution social outcome; `societyNpcWork(s,id,minutes,place)` records completed work. No new primary wallet or plan kind exists. Secondary contacts retain at most six completion handles `{id,from,to,startedAt,endsAt,place}` in `society.sessions`, referencing a real primary visit reservation and secondary `busyUntil`. Departure or conflicting commitments cancel the contact; completed contacts settle once.

`societyLearn(s,receiver,fact,api)` deduplicates episode IDs. Each fact stores `eventAt` (event), `learnedAt` (NPC receipt), and `disclosedAt` (player disclosure, zero while undisclosed). Delivery does not itself disclose information to the player. An actual call/talk/meeting exposes shareable facts; private facts never enter legacy profile disclosure or correction descriptors. Public fact rows expose `happenedAt`, player-facing `learnedAt`, `originActor`, previous source actor, credibility and hop count. These timestamps survive chronology rotation. A later completed obligation with the original witness can support bounded trust repair; it reverses only that episode's applied rumor penalty and never deletes a missed appointment or broken promise.

Eviction also updates sparse `society.retiredBefore[receiver][origin]` timestamps (at most 18×18), preventing an old sender from reacquiring the same forgotten episode and repeatedly damaging reputation. The watermark advances only when a fact leaves the bounded memory, so ordinary out-of-order reports and retained-record corrections remain possible. Unknown facts at or before a retired source timestamp, including simultaneous unknown episodes at that boundary, are considered stale. Evicting a record reverses only its still-active rumor penalty once; already corrected records add no further trust. Actual missed obligations remain in their original calendar/promise authorities. Disclosed memory decay has its own causal chronology row; private facts stay private.

Open offers expose `actor`, `start`, `end`, `venue`, expiry, keyed title/reason and existing calendar action IDs. There is exactly one canonical counter per offer, one finite revision, and no player commitment before acceptance. Both acceptance and attendance recheck the NPC's funds. Attendance debits that NPC's existing cash by the same venue fee (55/75/90 TL) once; player costs remain the canonical calendar cost. Ignoring an unaccepted offer incurs no broken-promise penalty. Secondary contact requests reuse `life:call:ID` and `society:ack:OFFER`.

After acceptance, ordinary calendar rescheduling preserves invitation provenance by following confirmed `replacesId` links. A pending replacement counter also inherits the NPC-funds check through its ancestry; attendance charges the replacement venue's actual fee. Future confirmed invitations retain their offer link even when history is pruned. When all eight retained offers are live, initiation pauses before creating another counter; only settled historical offers can be removed.

Public network rows are learned snapshots, never private edge references. Profiles expose only disclosed facts, known ties and last player-disclosure time. District rows expose current stock, service usage, bounded weekly effects, six accounting/history rows, next weekly expiry and applicable existing action IDs. `societyCost` descriptor metadata identifies food/material/service price and time changes for localized rendering. Signed zero is normalized before saveable weekly effects are stored.

## Root flow contract

`flow` is separately versioned and does not change the strict4emergent policy fields. Defaults never authorize new payments, debt, commitments, lease changes, relationships or automatic story choices. Existing explicit policies remain honored. Flow stores bounded routine-stop diagnostics and optional explicit safe-batching preferences. `getDecisionView(s,{actions?,society?}={})` returns `{urgent,opportunities,background,routine,goals,finance}`; UI may pass its already-cached action/public-society views to avoid duplicate computation. Items expose `{id,title,summary,why,dueAt,actionIds,target:{tab,npcId?,districtId?,eventId?,calendarDay?},source}`. It may use player-owned facts and public views only.

Routine diagnostic: `{code,reason,at,blocked,retryAllowed,stopKey,actionIds,target,performedCount}`. Stable stop keys depend on relevant state/policies and exclude revision/history/log noise. Unchanged known zero-time blockers cannot be blindly retried; relevant explicit controls and real recovery actions remain reachable. Days disabled by the player are never silently re-enabled. Already accepted calendar/work/travel constraints remain authoritative. Any optional continuation horizon must remain bounded and explicitly cancelable; no background clock or automatic high-stakes choice.

Monthly high-bank/debt traces reflect unoptimized player decisions. Show actual interest and available repayment/reserve choices, never silently pay debt. Separate existing contractual accrual from new borrowing and genuinely necessary financial review. A change that allows routine settlement must require explicit reviewable consent and still stop before unaffordable charges, new debt contracts, arrears, changed lease/partner/job conditions or budget-floor violations. Separate utility, principal-installment, rent-arrears-plan and business-overhead boundaries share this rule across all three batching paths. Consent cannot silently miss an installment, cancel an unaffordable plan or create business arrears; new plans and changed business commitments revoke it. No metric improvement from relabelling mandatory consent as waste.
