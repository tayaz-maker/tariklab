## Six playable loops

| Loop                                     | Authoritative sequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Proof through normal actions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Legal work and advancement               | Apply to a real local vacancy, fulfill scheduled shifts, credit earnings once, qualify or advance, then use those funds for rent, purchase or enterprise expenses. Training changes qualifications. Illness and missed shifts affect earnings through the same contract.                                                           | A player works, improves a qualification, and buys or leases from wages. A control without attendance earns no wage. A counterfactual with no vacancy or qualification requirement unmet cannot take the same offer.                                               |
| Join and advance in an organization      | Meet a recruiter during a confirmed time window, accept a recorded membership, take a task with specified requirements, complete it within the due window and receive a real result. Completed work changes trust and responsibility. Promotion unlocks tasks with new costs and obligations.                                      | A member earns a promotion after qualified tasks. A missed deadline changes trust, and replaying a completed task cannot pay again.                                                                                                                                |
| Competition and local influence          | Take a contract or purchase from a supplier, fulfill it with real stock and time, and receive payment from a counterparty's treasury. Repeated outcomes change bounded influence. Changes alter district demand, supply access, bid availability or prices through the district authority.                                         | Matched traces with and without a completed deal have different orders or costs in the affected district. The same transfer appears once in both applicable ledgers.                                                                                               |
| Incident, evidence and legal consequence | A timed unlawful action creates a specific incident when it takes effect. Present witnesses and matching records produce evidence under explicit rules. A report, inquiry and finding progress on scheduled days using that evidence. The decision can create a fine, repayment, restrictions or dismissal under the stated rules. | Compare an incident with a witness to an otherwise matched one without that witness. Losing access to a witness can affect corroboration. Reporting, review, payment and load/replay cannot manufacture evidence or duplicate sanctions.                           |
| Leave and establish another life         | End membership or let it lapse through an explicit action and date. Exit closes active tasks and offers, preserves earned balances and open claims, and changes access and known relationships. Legal work and lawful housing remain reachable when their requirements are met.                                                    | Leave with a pending obligation, move and work legally. After reload, an old contact sees the former relationship and outstanding claims remain payable.                                                                                                           |
| Family and estate                        | Link decisions and history to stable person IDs. At death, stop further personal action until the existing adult successor rule allows handover. Transfer or retain estate assets and established estate liabilities once; expire deceased-person role commitments and keep historical case records.                               | Pick two adult successors through the normal resolver, with property, company assets, debt, open compensation and known relationships. Compare the inherited estate to the predecessor's state and prove a second handover cannot copy property or make new money. |

## Place and economy boundary

Use `place = { regionId, districtId, venueId }` at the integration boundary. Existing İstanbul IDs `kadikoy`, `besiktas` and `sisli` keep their identity and use `s.location`. Use city-qualified IDs for new regional districts and an expansion-only place reference there. Derive legacy regional defaults from the region and real housing record. On the first relevant successful action the clone can persist that place without changing the imported save bytes. A district cannot also be a region, and a venue must belong to the current district.

The district workstream owns the registry, place and route resolution, resident locality and the read-only `getDistrictView(state, districtId)` projection. A shared internal district adapter can supply demand, rent pressure, stocks, job openings, service delay, security and influence. Only the resolver and scheduled ticks apply mutations. The UI receives the current public conditions and discovered relationships, with no access to hidden balances, undiscovered witnesses or sealed records. Travel uses agreed costs, times and venue hours. During transit a person cannot fulfill a task or be a witness at the destination before arrival.

Housing still uses the existing title/lease ledger. A property maps to one district and has one owner and at most one active lease. Employer and firm accounts use the current economy structures wherever they already represent the relevant payment. Stock moves between identified sources and sinks once, and wages leave the employer before reaching the employee. Organization accounts pay only from funded treasuries and cannot mirror household, business or municipal balances. Outside inflows such as deliveries or ordinary trade must carry source and reason. The same value cannot be credited through an action and its follow-up hook.

## Proposed persistence and visibility

| Record                  | Minimum authority and invariants                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `underworld.chapters`   | Bounded chapter IDs tied to a district. Each has treasury, stock and a bounded influence value. Finite source and sink accounting constrains contracts and competition.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `memberships` and tasks | Person ID, chapter ID, status, trust, responsibility, joined/left game minute and bounded open task records. Tasks use stable IDs and carry offer, accepted, due and resolved times. Proposals and status changes are observable only through the relevant contacts or records.                                                                                         |
| Incidents and evidence  | Each incident has actor, place, effective minute, incident type and source action ID or scheduled NPC action. Evidence links back to that incident and to its authentic witness, transaction, route or venue record. Keep observation date, source and corroboration distinct from when the player learned it.                                                          |
| Cases                   | One case per relevant incident with district authority, evidence references, next review day, status and decision. Opening, decision, review, satisfaction and closure have discrete transitions. Keep subject person ID and any estate-payable amount separate. A personal sanction expires or persists by its own rule and never transfers to a successor's identity. |
| Departures and estates  | A membership retains its former status and history. Continuing claims point to their liable person or household estate. Existing family people and ownership IDs remain authoritative. A successor may inherit property and documented estate debt, while rank and personal trust require their own relationship.                                                       |

Durable records are bounded by validation. Keep unresolved memberships, claims and cases until their disposition. Closed records can move into the existing bounded chronological history, with any still-referenced claim preserved. Record only transitions with an observable state cause. Public history uses the original subject at event time and localized display strings. Never regenerate history from the current occupant of a district, firm or household.

## Resolution and verification

- Add opt-in actions through the existing expansion coordinator in `expansion.mjs`. The engine checks eligibility, minutes and funds on original state, then runs and settles against its clone. Mark the actual effective minute in a single action owner. If completion semantics are necessary, finalize once after time advances and disallow completion after death. The same action cannot be issued twice for the same contract or case decision.
- Use existing day boundaries for scheduled task expiry, investigations, wages and local market updates where possible. A within-day event must share the existing time-advance path. Advance time across boundaries individually, with stable actor and record order. Preview or view functions never consume RNG. Prefer state-based triggers. Any new randomness consumes the existing deterministic source only at the declared authoritative step.
- Merge one source for rates, opportunity and effects. Rendering and action descriptions use the same quotes as execution. Write no balance, evidence or membership from the UI. Use original Turkish source messages with complete EN/PL presentation, preserving names and escaping imported text.
- Choose the next save schema explicitly before implementation. The suggested approach is an optional, validated phase-2 record under the opt-in expansion, with absent data derived read-only and materialized only in the successful cloned action. If exact validation or compatibility requires a schema change, plan a tested migration and rollback before adding fields. Gold remains byte-identical. Legacy v7 saves round-trip before normalization. Save locking, stale-tab checks, envelopes and engine identity remain in the existing persistence path.
- Set focused criteria for funded actions, delinquency, case and estate transitions, fixture read/round-trip, deterministic replay, valid empty and maximal histories, and malformed imports. Test every loop with the ordinary resolver and manual-versus-batched time progression. Verify desktop and mobile flows, all supported locales, opt-in loading and production package checks on exact candidate bytes.
